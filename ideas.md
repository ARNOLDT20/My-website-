# Design direction — Blaze Tech Hub

## Direction exploration

1. **Fireline Studio** — A calm, editorial technology hub with warm ember accents, deep ink surfaces, clear service groupings, and an inviting community tone. Probability: 0.08.
2. **Signal Grid** — A crisp, light-first engineering directory with modular linework, cool slate, and compact service cards. Probability: 0.06.
3. **Night Market** — A vivid, expressive digital culture portal with bold gradients, motion, and colorful project tiles. Probability: 0.03.

**Committed direction: Fireline Studio.** It keeps the energetic Blaze identity while making the experience calm, trustworthy, and easy to navigate. The site should feel like a shared front door for the team, members, and prospective clients—not an ad-heavy personal blog or a pretend authenticated dashboard.

## Design system

- **Design movement:** Contemporary digital studio with a restrained editorial dashboard sensibility.
- **Core principles:** One clear next step per section; describe the actual service before its flourish; keep public, member, and project paths easy to distinguish; never imply an integration, customer result, or private account capability that has not been implemented.
- **Color philosophy:** Near-black ink and graphite for structure, warm ivory for breathing room, ember orange for Blaze identity and primary actions, with a muted sea-glass accent for status and secondary actions. Use color as a navigation cue, not decoration everywhere.
- **Layout paradigm:** A compact sticky navigation, asymmetric hero with a handcrafted interface illustration, then a single-scroll hierarchy for services, ecosystem, member connection, process, FAQ, and contact. Responsive cards should collapse into a readable single column on narrow screens.
- **Signature elements:** A simple bolt/ember wordmark, small numbered section markers, crisp hairline borders, and a small “studio console” illustration built from real site content rather than a generic stock hero.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Keyboard-usable navigation and filters; visible focus states; direct WhatsApp contact actions with context prefilled; no autoplay, surprise popups, or fake sign-in interactions.
- **Animation:** Short, subtle hover and reveal transitions only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no looping decorative motion.
- **Typography system:** System sans-serif stack for reliable, fast rendering; expressive large heading scale paired with comfortable 16–18px body text and readable line lengths.
- **Brand essence:** A practical, ambitious technology collective connecting useful digital services with people who build and use them.
- **Brand voice:** Clear, warm, capable, grounded, and direct. Avoid inflated claims and unexplained jargon.
- **Wordmark / logo:** “BLAZE TECH” in bold uppercase with a minimal angular bolt/ember SVG mark; “T20 CLASSIC TECH” is presented as part of the wider ecosystem, not as an unrelated competing brand.
- **Signature brand color:** Ember orange `#F36B3F` against deep ink `#111827`.

## Asset decision

The selected surface is a service and community hub. Use simple inline SVG interface icons and CSS-built product panels instead of stock photography; no real people or product photographs are required. Avoid adding external image dependencies to the first load.
