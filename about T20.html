ABOUT BLAZE TECH / T20 CLASSIC TECH

A team, a community,
and a shared direction.

Blaze Tech brings digital services, project work, technology learning, and community connections together in one ecosystem.

T20 CLASSIC TECH is a youth-driven digital platform based in Tanzania, focused on smart tech education, innovation, and digital empowerment. Its original promise—Smart Tech. Smarter You.—is still the foundation: make useful technology and practical learning easier for everyone to access.

The T20 archive brings back beginner-friendly phone and app guidance, WhatsApp tips, web-development explainers, code tutorials, and digital basics. We keep older guides clearly labeled as archive content because app menus and device settings change over time. Explore the T20 guide library ↗

Blaze Tech extends that learning community into a wider set of digital projects and services—from BLAZE-MD and Blaze Tech Academy to software development, automation, hosting, and digital-business tools. We want members, learners, and collaborators to find a clear next step and talk directly with the team.
